Small machine. Big voice?

The challenge is not to call a modern voice service. The 65C02 must turn the text into sound through the real Mockingboard interface, at native 1x speed.

3RIC has 36 KiB of always-mapped RAM, 48 KiB below I/O when BASIC ROM is disabled, and additional language-card banking with ROM/input/interrupt constraints. The full brief explains the difference.

Something worth keeping.

Each entry should leave a type-and-speak app, a reusable speech routine and source you can inspect. Original pronunciation and synthesis are required; existing 3RIC hardware-access helpers are allowed with attribution.

This round targets 3RIC. Apple II ports are welcome later, but exporting a WOZ is not a compatibility test.
