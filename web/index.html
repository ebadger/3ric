65C02 emulator core (C++) + WozLib compiled to WebAssembly via Emscripten. Boots the real 512KB ROM, renders Apple-II text/hi-res to the canvas, and feeds keystrokes through the memory-mapped keyboard ($C000). Click the screen and type. “Load Lode Runner” launches the romdisk game.
