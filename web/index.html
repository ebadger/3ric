65C02 emulator core (C++) + WozLib compiled to WebAssembly via Emscripten. Boots the real 512KB ROM, renders Apple-II text/hi-res to the canvas, and feeds keystrokes through the memory-mapped keyboard ($C000). Click the screen and type. “Boot Disk” boots the bundled 5.25″ floppy (or pick a .woz with “Insert”) through the Disk II ROM at $C600. “Mount SD” enters the DOS shell (prompt “>”) and lists the micro-SD card — load games and programs from there. “Load .PRG” loads a raw program image at the given hex address and runs it, exactly like BRUN FILE.PRG <addr> on the hardware (or deep-link one with ?prg=programs/hello.prg&org=0800). “Speed” sets the CPU clock (1× ≈ 1 MHz; “Max” runs as fast as the frame allows).
