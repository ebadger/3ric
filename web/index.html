Pick a sample or write 65C02 assembly, then Assemble & Run.
65C02 emulator core (C++) + WozLib compiled to WebAssembly via Emscripten. Boots the real 512KB ROM, renders Apple-II text/hi-res to the canvas, and feeds keystrokes through the memory-mapped keyboard ($C000). Click the screen and type. “Boot Disk” boots the bundled 5.25″ floppy (or pick a .woz with “Insert”) through the Disk II ROM at $C600. “Mount SD” enters the DOS shell (prompt “>”) and lists the micro-SD card — load games and programs from there. “Load .PRG” loads a raw program image at the given hex address and runs it, exactly like BRUN FILE.PRG <addr> on the hardware (or deep-link one with ?prg=programs/hello.prg&org=0800). “Speed” sets the CPU clock (native 1× ≈ 1.57 MHz — the 25.175 MHz VGA dot clock ÷ 16; “Max” runs as fast as the frame allows). The Assembler panel assembles 65C02 source in your browser (the same assembler the project uses on the command line) and runs it just like “Load .PRG” — pick a sample, edit it, Assemble & Run (or Ctrl+Enter), and download the resulting .PRG to BRUN on hardware. Hit Share to copy a one-click link to your program (a short ?src= for the built-in samples, or your source inlined in ?code=).
Building with an AI tool? Point it at llms.txt — a machine-readable 65C02 codegen guide — or see CONTRIBUTING.
