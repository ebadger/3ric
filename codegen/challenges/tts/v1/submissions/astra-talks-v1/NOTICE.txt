The AY amplitude calibration numbers in generate.mjs are hardware constants
from emulator/Badger6502VMLib/ay38910.cpp at the frozen 3RIC baseline.
This is an extracted calibration table, NOT that sound-chip implementation
and NOT speech/voice data. That source carries the following notice:

AY-3-8910 sound generator adapted from floooh/chips ay38910.h at
commit 9371bfcb8478aec05ad10a1293206363373c1489.

Changes for 3ric: converted to a C++ class, removed generic pin masks,
snapshots, and unconnected I/O callbacks, and separated chip ticking from
host PCM sampling.
